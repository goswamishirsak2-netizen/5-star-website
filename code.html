The Origin

Our journey begins in the shaded understory of ancient rainforests. We source only the rarest Criollo and Trinitario cacao beans, selected for their complex flavor profiles and deep, resonant aromas. Each pod is hand-harvested at the exact moment of peak ripeness.

Sustainable Harvesting

Cultivated in harmony with the environment, ensuring the preservation of the rainforest canopy and the livelihoods of our generational farming partners.

"The true magic lies not just in the ingredients, but in the precise calibration of temperature and time."

02

The Alchemy

Crafting the perfect confection is an exercise in precise alchemy. We begin with a foundation of slow-whipped nougat, aerated to achieve a cloud-like texture that yields instantly upon tasting.

The Golden Ribbon

Over this, we drape a ribbon of deeply caramelized sugar, touched with hand-harvested sea salt. The caramel is cooked until it reaches the exact temperature where sweet transitions into a complex, roasted resonance.

The Final Enrobing

Finally, the layered core is enrobed in a thick shell of our signature 72% dark chocolate, sealing the delicate textures within a crisp, satisfying snap.
